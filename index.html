Third-person learning

Observe before assuming.

Questions should normally describe a friend, colleague or another person rather than assume something is happening to the learner.

Example

Someone who is usually very talkative becomes unusually quiet. What might a thoughtful friend do first?

Keep it light

Short questions, small steps and occasional gentle humour — never jokes about violence, trauma or danger.
